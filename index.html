Privacy Policy for PDF Scanner

Effective date: October 12, 2026

PDF Scanner ("the app") is built to respect your privacy. This policy explains what data the app collects and why.

Documents you scan

Your scans stay on your device. Scan images and PDFs are processed locally and never uploaded to our servers.

Analytics and crash reporting

The app does not use third-party analytics or crash reporting.

Accounts

The app has no user accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect your name, email, or contact information.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store and by RevenueCat for subscription management. We do not see or store your payment card details. RevenueCat processes purchase receipts and a device identifier to manage your "pro" entitlement. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Data not collected

The app does not collect location data, contacts, or any advertising identifiers. The only photos accessed are the scans you choose to capture.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.
